Membership Tiers

INTM membership opens doors to exclusive resources, professional recognition, peer networks, and career advancement opportunities.

Two Pathways

Two Pathways to Membership

Become a member of the Institute of Nutritional Therapy Management (INTM) through either of the following routes.

Route 1

Training & Examination Route

Complete any approved INTM training programme and successfully pass the required examination to qualify for membership.

Explore Courses Enquire
Route 2

Direct Membership Route

Gain membership based on your academic qualifications and relevant professional experience in nutrition, health, or related fields.

Experience guidance
  • Associate Member: Typically 0–1 year experience (suitable for early-career applicants).
  • Full Member: Typically 2–5 years relevant professional experience.
  • Fellow Member: Typically 5–10 years experience with evidence of leadership/impact.
  • Chartered Member: Typically 7–10+ years experience with proven expertise and credibility.
