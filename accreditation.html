Accreditation & Quality

Our programs are built on evidence-based practice and structured quality assurance to support professional competence and public trust.

Commitment to Excellence

INTM maintains a standards-led approach to course design, assessment, and professional recognition. For accreditation enquiries and verification guidance, please contact the Institute.

What Accreditation Means

Accreditation is our commitment to structured learning outcomes, transparent assessment, ethical practice, and continuous improvement. It supports public trust and helps learners and employers understand the competence expected of certified practitioners.

Curriculum Quality

Clear outcomes, evidence-based content, and practical application to real-world settings.

Assessment Integrity

Consistent assessment standards that verify competence, not just attendance.

Professional Ethics

Guidance on ethical practice, scope of work, and responsible communication.

Quality Assurance Framework

INTM Global maintains a structured quality assurance approach for program delivery and professional recognition. This includes periodic reviews of learning outcomes, assessment methods, and learner support systems.

  • Defined learning outcomes and competency expectations per program.
  • Documented assessment methods and performance criteria.
  • Ongoing feedback loops from learners, faculty, and partners.
  • Continuous improvement through review cycles and updates.

Accreditation FAQ

Answers to common questions about standards, recognition, and verification.

Does accreditation mean a program is “government approved”?
Accreditation refers to the Institute’s standards and quality processes. If a program carries additional external recognition, it will be stated clearly in the program information.
How can I verify a certificate or membership?
Use the Verify Credentials page to search by credential ID or member details. If you need help, contact the Institute.
Can I request an accreditation letter or confirmation?
Yes. Send an enquiry via the contact page with the details of the program and the information you need for confirmation.
How often are standards reviewed?
Standards are reviewed periodically based on program cycles, new evidence, and feedback from faculty, learners, and partners.
