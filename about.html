About INTM Global

INTM Global exists to empower nutritionists, dietitians, health coaches, and allied health professionals with world-class training, cutting-edge research, and a thriving professional community.

Mission

Bridging Nutrition Science with Real-World Practice

We focus on practical, evidence-based education and professional standards that translate into measurable improvements in individual and population health.

For partnership, research, or training enquiries, email direct@intmglobal.org.
