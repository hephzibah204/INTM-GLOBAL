Our Story

Bridging the Gap in Nutritional Care

Founded on the principle that evidence-based nutrition is the cornerstone of preventative and therapeutic health, INTM Global provides a structured framework for nutrition professionals to excel. We recognized the need for a unified standard of practice and a hub for continuous professional development that addresses the unique health challenges of the 21st century.

🎯

Our Mission

To advance the standard of nutritional therapy management through accredited education, pioneering research, and professional advocacy.

👁️

Our Vision

A world where evidence-based nutritional therapy is integrated into mainstream healthcare systems to improve population health outcomes.

💎

Core Values

Integrity, Scientific Rigour, Community Impact, and Professional Excellence in everything we do.

Science-Led, Patient-Centred
